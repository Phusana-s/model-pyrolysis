About the Platform

To enhance accessibility and promote collaborative engagement within the scientific community, we have designed and implemented the endp web server. This user-friendly platform is openly accessible online at http://endp.pythonanywhere.com. Within this framework, we have meticulously provided detailed, step-by-step guidelines to facilitate users in effectively utilizing the endp web server for obtaining specific and desired research outcomes. The unrestricted availability of this resource contributes to the seamless integration of advanced computational tools, fostering a conducive environment for scientific exploration and discovery.

Guidelines for Operation
Step 1: Access the web server by navigating to http://endp.pythonanywhere.com. Upon initiation, the user interface on the endp web server will be displayed on the computer screen, presenting the primary interface.
Step 2: Kindly populate the properties of materials and process condition factors in the designated fields. Subsequently, initiate the process by selecting the "Output" button situated at the lower section. The resulting outcome will be the diameter of nano fiber value.
Should you encounter any challenges in utilization or wish to provide suggestions, please feel free to reach out to us via email at sombsuk@kku.ac.th
